About Durgeshwari Jewellers

Durgeshwari Jewellers is a jewellery showroom in Kanjurmarg East, Mumbai, offering traditional Indian gold jewellery for weddings, festivals and everyday celebrations.

Our designs are inspired by Indian heritage, from the poth and traditional necklaces to bangles, jhumkas and men's rings. Visit the showroom to see the collection in person.
